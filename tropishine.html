Meet Tropishine

A revolutionary semi-organic shampoo turning plantain peel waste into premium scalp care. Affordable, eco-friendly, and effective.

Order Now Learn More

Executive Summary

Tropishine is our novel solution to solving a multidimensional problem in our local community.

By collecting plantain peel waste and processing it into a primary active ingredient with other organic materials; Neem, Cloves and Rosemary, we produce a semi-organic shampoo. With a production capacity of 60 bottles per week, our cost of production is ₦540 per unit, and retail price is ₦1,000, generating a margin of ₦460.

We have produced 200+ bottles, sold 160, and generated ₦160,000 in revenue. We've distributed through our local school market retailer, saloons, beauty shops, exhibitions, and through Enactus members to the entire university community and beyond. We secured an active partnership with the Ikere LGA Chairman, and pending partnerships with Ekiti state Ministry of Trade and Investment, Ministry of Youth, and Ministry of Education respectively.

Our impact is threefold: students gaining access to affordable scalp treatment, agro-waste diverted from open decomposition thus reducing greenhouse gas emissions and protecting public health, and a self-sustaining venture generating income within the community it serves.

The Problem

Banana and plantain peels make up 30-40% of the fruit's total weight. Across Ekiti State, in every market, farm, and community, these peels are discarded daily into open spaces and waste dumps. When they decompose, they release methane, a greenhouse gas 25 times more potent than CO2. They attract flies and other pests, create unpleasant odours, and contribute to environmental pollution. This is happening every day. It is urgent, visible, and addressable, yet it is being ignored.

In the same communities generating this waste, scalp infections are rampant. The infections spread easily through shared combs, reused barbing tools without sterilization, and borrowed towels and pillows. Solutions do exist—homemade local remedies and medications are available. However, neither option fits naturally into a daily hygiene routine. What was missing was not a cure, but a better option: something economical, efficient, easy to use, and eco-friendly that sits in the routine where prevention actually happens.

Our Impact

People

Tropishine has directly reached 200+ students and young people in Ikere-Ekiti, providing affordable semi-organic hair care that supports scalp health, hair growth, and deep conditioning. Through their households, the impact extends to 700+ additional individuals.

Economic

Tropishine has generated ₦268,000 in economic value, including ₦108,000 in local production spend and ₦160,000 from 160 units sold. A ₦73,600 surplus has been returned to the venture with one local retail partner onboarded. Production runs at 60 bottles weekly, with a projected ₦2.88M annual revenue at full scale.

Planet

Waste Repurposing: Tropishine converts discarded plantain peels from markets, farms, and households across Ekiti State into its primary active ingredient.

Emission Reduction: This reduces agro-waste decomposition and helps prevent methane emissions, a greenhouse gas 25x more potent than CO2.
